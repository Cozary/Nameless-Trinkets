Unwanted or duplicate Trinkets can be **recycled** to obtain **Unknown Fragments**.  

### How to Obtain Unknown Fragments  
- **Right-click a Trinket on an Amethyst Block** to break it down into **Unknown Fragments**.  
- If the Amethyst Block is unavailable in your version, **left-clicking a Trinket on an Enchanting Table** will have the same effect.

### Crafting a Mysterious Trinket  
Once you collect **9 Unknown Fragments**, you can **craft a Mysterious Trinket**, which grants you a random Trinket when used.
