#Dying Star

##How it works:
The **Dying Star** trinket grants the following effect:

- Right-clicking a trinket on it will devour it and cause it to disappear. Giving the _Dying Star_ other trinkets increases a random attribute by a random amount.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.

## Notes:
- Pressing **Left Ctrl** on the trinket's tooltip will show you all the attributes and their values.
