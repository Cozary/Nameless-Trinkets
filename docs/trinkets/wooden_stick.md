# Wooden Stick

## How it works:
The **Wooden Stick** trinket grants the following effects:

- The trinket intercepts incoming damage and completely cancels it, but this effect is subject to a cooldown period.
- After the trinket negates damage, it enters a cooldown period during which it cannot be used again until it resets.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Cooldown**: The amount of time, in ticks, that the trinket is on cooldown after use.
