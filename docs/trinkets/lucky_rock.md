# Lucky Rock

## How it works:
The **Lucky Rock** trinket grants the following effect:

- Gives players a chance to obtain random items when breaking `Stone Blocks` or blocks listed in "Block List".

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Percentage of Obtaining**: The probability that the player will receive an item upon breaking a stone block.
- **Item List**: A list of item strings representing items that the player may receive.
- **Block List**: A list of block strings representing blocks that the player can break and may receive items.

## Notes:
- The effect is only triggered when breaking **stone** blocks.
