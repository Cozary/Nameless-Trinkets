#Dragon'sEye

##How it Works
The **Dragon's Eye** trinket grants the following effects:

- Applies `Blindness` to the player.
- Applies to Nearby hostile enemies `Glowing` in a *light purple* color.

## Configuration
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Blindness:** Configurable duration in ticks.
- **Radius:** Defines the range in blocks to detect entities.
