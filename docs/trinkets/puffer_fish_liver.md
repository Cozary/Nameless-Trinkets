# Puffer Fish Liver

## How it works:
The **Puffer Fish Liver** trinket grants the following effects:

- Allows players to apply a poison effect to enemies when they inflict damage.
- Prevents the player from being poisoned.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Chance to Apply Poison**: The probability that the trinket will apply the **Poison** effect when the player inflicts damage.
- **Poison Time**: The duration (in ticks) that the **Poison** effect lasts on the affected entity.
- **Poison Level**: The level of the **Poison** effect, which determines the strength of the poison damage.
