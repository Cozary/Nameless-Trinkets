# Callus

###How it works:
The **Callus** trinket grants the following effects:

- Reduces incoming damage of the following types:
	- General damage (everything).
	- Fall damage.
	- Cactus.
	- Falling anvils.
	- Hot floors.
	- Sweet berry bushes.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Fall Damage Reduction Percentage**: This indicates when fall damage is reduced as a percentage.
- **General Damage Reduction Percentage**: This indicates how much damage is reduced as a percentage.
