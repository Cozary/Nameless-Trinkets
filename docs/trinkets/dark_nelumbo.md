#Dark Nelumbo

##How it works:
The **Dark Nelumbo** trinket grants the following effects:

- Allows the player to stand on **lava** as if it were a solid block.
- If you press the shift key you will sink.
- If the lava reaches your eyes you will be propelled upwards.
- If you are on fire you stop being on fire.

#Configuration
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.

## Notes:
- You will not take damage from the lava.
