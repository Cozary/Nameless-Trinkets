#SpeedForce

##How it works:
The **Speed ​​Force** trinket grants the following effect:

- Increases the player's movement speed.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Speed ​​Multiplier Percentage**: The percentage increase in the player's movement speed.
- **Speed ​​Force Particles**: Whether or not you want the player to release particles.

## Notes:
- It also generates **cloud particles** around the player as they move, visually representing the speed boost.
- FOV settings are changed from `nameless-trinkets-common.tmol`.
