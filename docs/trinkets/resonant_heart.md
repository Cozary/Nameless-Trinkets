#Resonant Heart

##How it works:
The **Resonant Heart** trinket grants the following effect:

- Taking damage has a chance to release a Sonic Boom.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Chance To Activate Probability**: Determines the chance for the trinket to activate upon taking damage.
- **Sonic Boom Damage**: Determines the damage it deals.

## Notes:
- As with the Warden's Sonic Boom, this only affects one entity: the attacker.
