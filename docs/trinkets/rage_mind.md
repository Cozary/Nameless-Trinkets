# Rage Mind

## How it works:
The **Rage Mind** trinket grants the following effects:

- When receiving damage from an entity the trinket marks it
- The marked entities will glow.
- Marked entities will take more damage from you.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Damage Multiplier Percentage**: Controls how much more damage the player deals to the revenge target.
- **Range**: The effective range in blocks for detecting and applying effects to revenge targets.

## Notes:
- The entity that the player is seeking revenge on, defined by its entity type.
