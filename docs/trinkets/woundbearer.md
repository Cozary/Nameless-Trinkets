#Woundbearer

##How it works:
The **Woundbearer** trinket grants the following effect:

- A percentage of the damage the player takes is stored in the trinket. Your attack damage increases based on the amount stored.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Damage Conversion Percentage**: The percentage of how much damage is dealt to the player is stored in the trinket.
