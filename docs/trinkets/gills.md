# Gills

## How it works:
The **Gills** trinket grants the following effects:

- Allows the player to breathe underwater indefinitely.
- If the player is out of water the air supply will gradually decrease, if the player runs out of air completely they will take damage and `Blindness`.
- The trinket can only be removed underwater.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Blindness When Choking**: This setting determines whether the player is inflicted with blindness when their air supply is depleted.
- **Choking Damage**: This specifies how much damage the player will take when their air supply is depleted.
- **Air Reduction Speed**: The rate at which the player’s air supply depletes when outside of water.
