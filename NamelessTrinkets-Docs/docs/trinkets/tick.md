# Tick

## How it works:
The **Tick** trinket grants the following effects:

- Applies if the entity has more than half of its life and its maximum life is more than 49.
- causes damage to the entity.
- The player is dealt both damage and hunger.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Range to Activate**: The radius in which the trinket searches for nearby targets to apply the effect.
- **Entity Damage Percentage**: The percentage of the entity's maximum health that is dealt as damage.
- **Player Damage Percentage**: The percentage of the player's maximum health that is dealt as damage.
- **Player Hunger Percentage**: The percentage of the player's food level used to exhaust the player when the trinket activates.

### Notes:
- The trinket **can be unequipped** while the player is on fire or in creative mode.
