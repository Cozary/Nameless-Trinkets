# Vampire Blood

## How it works:
The **Vampire Blood** trinket grants the following effects:

- When the player kills an entity, they heal for a percentage of the entity's maximum health. 
- The trinket makes the player vulnerable to sunlight. If the player is outside during the day and can see the sky, they take damage.
- The trinket boosts the player's attack damage by a percentage.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Healing Percentage**: The percentage of the killed entity’s maximum health that is healed.
- **Sun Damage**: The amount of damage the player takes when exposed to sunlight during the day.
- **Damage Multiplier Percentage**: The percentage increase in attack damage provided by the trinket.
