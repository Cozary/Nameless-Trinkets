# Ice Cube

## How it works:
The **Ice Cube** trinket grants the following effects:

- When dealing damage, affected entities are applied `Slowness`.
- When walking, nearby water blocks turn into `Frosted Ice`.
- The player becomes immune to freezing damage.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Slowness Time**: The duration of the slowness effect applied to the entity when the player attacks.
- **Slowness Level**: The level of the slowness effect.
- **Frost Walker Level**: The level of frost walker the player has, determining the radius for frosted ice creation.
- **Inmune to Freezing**: Whether the player is immune to freeze damage.
