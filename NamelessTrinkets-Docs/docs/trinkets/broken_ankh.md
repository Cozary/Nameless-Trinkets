#BrokenAnkh

##How it works.
The **Broken Ankh** trinket grants the following effect:

- When the player is about to die, acts as a _Totem_ _of_ _Undying_. The trinket goes into cooldown after being used.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Cooldown**: This is the time in ticks until it can be used again.


## Notes:
- If the player logs in or logs out, the cooldown for the trinket is handled accordingly.
