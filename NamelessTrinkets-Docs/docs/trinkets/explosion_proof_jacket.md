# Explosion Proof Jacket

## How it works:
The **Explosion Proof Jacket** trinket grants the following effects:

- Reduces the damage taken from explosions.
- After the explosion, the trinket causes the player to drop a **TNT** item at their location.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Blast Damage Percentage Reduction**: The percentage by which the explosion damage is reduced.

## Notes:
- The dropped **TNT** item is **invulnerable**.
