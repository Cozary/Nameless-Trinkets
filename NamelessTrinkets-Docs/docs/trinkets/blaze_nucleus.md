#Blaze Nucleus

##How it works:
The **Blaze Nucleus** trinket grants the following effects:

- When the player attacks an enemy, the enemy is set on fire for an amount of time. Also, if the player is on fire, he stops being on fire.
- The player gains fire damage reduction.

## Configuration:
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.
- **Set Enemy in Fire Ticks**: The number of fire ticks to apply to enemies when they are attacked.
- **Fire Damage Reduction Percentage**: The percentage of fire damage reduction.
