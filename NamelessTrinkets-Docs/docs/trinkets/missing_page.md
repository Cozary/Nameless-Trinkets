# Missing Page  

## How it works  
The **Missing Page  ** trinket grants the following effect:

- When you take damage from an entity you may deal a percentage of its maximum life as damage to nearby entities.  

## Configuration  
- **Is Enable**: Determines whether the trinket's effect is active or not.  
- **Activation Percentage**: The chance that the trinket will activate when the player is damaged.  
- **Radius in Blocks**: The area around the player where valid targets can be affected.  
- **Percentage of Damage**: The portion of the target's maximum health that will be dealt as damage upon activation.  

## Notes  
- The effect only applies to **non-player** entities that are not invulnerable.  
- When activated, soul particles are displayed around affected enemies.  
- The trinket does not trigger if the source of damage is another player.
